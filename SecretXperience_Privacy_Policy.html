We act as the data controller for personal data processed through this website and its associated services, as defined under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) 2016/679.

Our platform operates as a directory and marketplace — we connect independent adult service providers, venue operators, and content creators with adult clients. We do not employ service providers and are not a party to the services delivered between listed individuals and their clients.

04 Financial transaction data

SecretXperience.eu does not store your payment card details. All card data is handled exclusively by Stripe (PCI DSS Level 1 certified), the world's leading payment processor. When you enter card details on our checkout, that information goes directly and securely to Stripe — our servers never see it.

Provider payouts

Service providers and creators who receive payments through SecretXperience are onboarded via Stripe Connect. Stripe collects and stores their bank account details, tax identification number, and identity documents for KYC (Know Your Customer) compliance under EU AML law. SecretXperience receives only payout confirmation — the underlying financial details remain with Stripe.

Anti-money laundering (AML) obligations

Under Belgian law implementing EU Directive 2015/849 (AMLD), we are required to monitor transactions for suspicious activity and, where thresholds are met, report to the Financial Intelligence Processing Unit (CTIF-CFI). Transaction records are retained for a minimum of 5 years for this purpose. This is a legal obligation we cannot waive regardless of user preference.

Retention

Financial transaction records are retained for 7 years from the date of transaction, in compliance with Belgian accounting law (Wetboek van Vennootschappen en Verenigingen). You may request access to your transaction history at any time.

05 Identity & property verification

Provider identity verification

Service providers who wish to display a ✓ Verified badge must complete identity verification through Yoti / AgeID, an EU-accredited identity verification provider. This process involves submitting a government-issued ID and a liveness check (selfie).

Important: SecretXperience.eu never receives, stores, or views your identity document. The document and biometric data go directly to Yoti. We only receive a cryptographic confirmation token — "identity verified, age 18+" — along with an opaque reference code. We cannot reverse-engineer your identity from this token.

06 Age verification

SecretXperience.eu is an adults-only platform. We are required under the EU Digital Services Act (DSA) Article 28 to implement robust measures preventing minors from accessing adult content. We apply a layered age verification approach:

ActionVerification level
Browsing the platformSelf-declaration at age gate (cookie stored)
Creating an accountEmail confirmation + self-declaration
Messaging a providerVerified account required
Making a booking or subscribingAccount + valid payment method (Stripe age indicators)
Posting a listing (provider / creator)Full ID verification via Yoti / AgeID
Withdrawing earningsFull KYC via Stripe Connect

The age gate self-declaration is stored as a browser cookie for 30 days. It does not contain any personal data — only a boolean flag ("user confirmed 18+"). Clearing your cookies removes it and the gate will reappear on your next visit.

12 Children

SecretXperience.eu is strictly for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone under 18 years of age. If we discover that an account belongs to a minor, we will immediately suspend the account, delete all associated data, and report the matter to the relevant authorities if legally required.

If you believe a minor has accessed or registered on this platform, please contact us immediately at safeguarding@secretxperience.eu. We treat all such reports as urgent.
