### **Lesson 32: Use White Background** *(49:21 – 49:53)*

* **Lesson Number & Title:** 32 - Use White Background
* **The Rule in One Sentence:** Text printed on a white background with dark lettering is significantly easier to read at a distance than dark backgrounds with white lettering ("reverse print").
* **Concrete Examples, Studies, Numbers, and Ads:**
  * **Study Experiment:** Participants tested reading performance on two test pages displaying the text **"Difficult to read"**:
    1. Black text on white paper.
    2. White text on black paper.
  * **Results:** Black text on white paper was easily readable from a distance, while white text on black paper forced participants to physically step closer to decipher it.
* **Exact Advice on How to Apply It:** Avoid using dark or black backgrounds ("reverse text") for body text; stick to dark text on a white background.
