### 4. TIPS & RULES
* **Specify Camera Framing:** *"Including things like '3/4 wide-angle view' gives the AI context as to what you want to see."* Skipping framing directives results in unpredictable camera placement.
* **Use Neutral Grey Backgrounds:** *"If you do white, sometimes it tends to be overexposed when using as a reference... Grey is a neutral tone that doesn't give that overexposure."*
* **Test Multiple Image Models:** Try different models on the same prompt to find the style and accuracy needed before generating final video references.
