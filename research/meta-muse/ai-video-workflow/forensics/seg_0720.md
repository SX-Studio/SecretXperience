#### B. Prompting Format Structure (12:27–14:23)
* **[GOAL]**: Create a 15-second cinematic scene where Dan walks through a futuristic AI control room, takes a remote from his pocket, and shuts down the AI system with one click. Build tension during his approach, then finish with a quiet, confident moment.
* **[CHARACTER AND REFERENCES]**:
  * **Dan** – Handsome 26-year-old man. Match his face and hair to attached reference. Black jacket, plain white T-shirt, dark trousers, black boots. Moves calmly and confidently.
  * **Location** – Control room architecture, server racks, central AI machine. Preserve layout across all shots.
  * **Remote** – Small matte-black remote with one circular red button. Starts inside Dan's right jacket pocket.
* **[SCENE]**: Night inside a large industrial AI control room. Central aisle leads toward AI machine with tall server racks on both sides. Blue screens illuminate the room. Thin haze catches light. Warm emergency light above entrance outlines Dan's shoulders. Dan is the only person present.
* **[STAGES]**:
  * **STAGE 1 (0–4 SECONDS) | THE APPROACH**: Medium side-angle tracking shot at waist height. Dan walks down central aisle toward AI machine. Expression is serious. Cut while walking to close-up of feet.
  * **STAGE 2 (4–6 SECONDS) | THE FINAL STEPS**: Low-angle close-up of Dan's boots. Takes final two steps. Blue reflections slide across leather. Stops with both feet firmly planted. Cut to upper body.
  * **STAGE 3 (6–8 SECONDS) | THE REACH**: Medium close-up from Dan's front-right side. Lowers right hand into jacket pocket. Hand begins to withdraw.
  * **STAGE 4 (8–15 SECONDS) | THE SHUTDOWN**: Close-up of Dan's right hand and pocket. Pulls remote completely out of pocket, raises to waist height. Thumb rests on red button. Says: *"Let's give AI a break,"* then presses button once. AI screens go dark.
* **[VISUAL STYLE]**: Photorealistic science-fiction thriller. Realistic skin, fabric, metal, reflections. Cool blue machine light contrasts warm entrance light. Smooth camera movements, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, natural motion blur.
* **[AUDIO]**:
  * **Music**: Low electronic music builds during Dan's approach, stops when button is pressed.
  * **Sound effects**: Steady server hum, heavy footsteps, jacket fabric moving, crisp button click. Machinery winds down.
  * **Dialogue**: *"Let's give AI a break."*
* **[MAINTAIN CONSISTENCY]**: Keep Dan's face, outfit, screen direction, room layout, and remote identical across every shot. Match body position and hand movement across cuts. Machine shuts down only after button press.

### 4. Tips & Rules Stated
* **Prompting Framework**: Always breakdown detailed prompts into explicit sections: `[GOAL]`, `[CHARACTER AND REFERENCES]`, `[SCENE]`, `[STAGES]`, `[VISUAL STYLE]`, `[AUDIO]`, and `[MAINTAIN CONSISTENCY]`.
* **Prompt Length / Granularity**: Breakdown the scene into timestamped stages (`0-4s`, `4-6s`, `6-8s`, `8-15s`) to give the AI precise camera angle and action instructions per shot transition.
* **Consistency Enforcer**: Explicitly add a consistency instruction clause at the end of prompt templates to keep character clothing, facial features, and background elements identical across camera cuts.
* **Leverage LLMs**: Do not write complex structured prompts manually; load a custom skill file (`.md`) into your LLM and dictate your scene ideas via voice-to-text to automatically format prompts.
