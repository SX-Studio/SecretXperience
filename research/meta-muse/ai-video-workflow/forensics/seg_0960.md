### 4. Tips, Rules & Mistakes to Avoid
* *"Keep in mind, you don't just want to use the first result. Now we're just going to re-iterate and get it exactly the way we want it to be."*
* *"Not all of your generation's first try will be best. That's why you want to use something like a draft mode that, if you like it, you can later upscale this and you don't burn through these credits."*
* *"If you notice something is wrong here, you can change your prompt, generate it again, and then you might get a better result. And this might take you like two or three tries, or maybe even more in the beginning."*
* Why use Draft Mode: Saves credits when experimenting with complex prompts before committing to high-resolution (1080p) upscaling.
