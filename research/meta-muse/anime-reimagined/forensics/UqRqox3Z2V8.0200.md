### 2. Generation Forensics

*   **Primary Generator Family:** **Runway Gen-3 Alpha / Kling AI combination**. 
    *   *Facial consistency & motion physics:* Characters like Escanor exhibit the classic "hyper-detailed skin texture with slight plastic sheen" typical of Runway Gen-3 text-to-video / image-to-video pipelines. 
    *   *Artifacts:* Minor morphing in facial hair and teeth during dialogue (e.g., Merlin at 03:41, Escanor at 03:20). 
    *   *Monster/Armor shots:* Galland's armor has consistent metallic and rocky textures across cuts, indicating strong image-to-video reference seeding (likely Midjourney v6 base images animated via Kling or Runway).
*   **Composites vs. Native Generation:** Complex scenes involving giant scales (Escanor towering over Galland at 04:29) are multi-layer composites: foreground AI-generated giant Escanor plates composited over a background plate with matched lighting, smoke elements, and color grading.

### 3. Look & Aesthetic (80s Dark Fantasy / Tokusatsu Treatment)

*   **Aspect Ratio & Resolution:** 4:3 letterbox at 1440x1080, mimicking vintage 80s television broadcasts or full-frame VHS masters.
*   **Film Stock Emulation:** Heavy 16mm/35mm film grain overlay, pronounced gate weave (subtle jitter), and edge halation/bloom around bright highlights (especially sun flares behind Escanor's axe at 04:25).
*   **Color Grade:** Heavily stylized teal-and-orange palette. Shadow areas crushed into deep, tinted blacks; midtones pushed toward warm copper/bronze (skin tones and armor); highlights blown out with warm golden sun flare.
*   **Vignette & Aberration:** Dark, heavy vignette framing shots; mild chromatic aberration (color fringing) on high-contrast edges to simulate vintage anamorphic lenses.
