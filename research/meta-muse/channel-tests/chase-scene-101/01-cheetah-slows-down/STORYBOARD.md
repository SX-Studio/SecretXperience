## Video direction

- **Look:** "Field Journal × Telemetry" (see frame.md). Warm ivory paper with a faint graph grid on every frame (its own full-bleed clip layer). Pen-and-ink public-domain plates (multiply on paper). Hand-written Caveat field notes with thin wobbly leader lines. Compass-drafted hairlines and rings. A modern GPS layer drawn over the journal in ONE signal red (#C2412D), only for data: tracks, the speed needle, key numbers.
- **Audience:** older viewers. Large type (nothing essential under 34 px), one idea per frame, high contrast ink on paper, slow, confident motion. Visible source chips on factual frames.
- **Motion grammar:** documentary calm: ink wipes, self-drawing lines, watercolor washes bleeding in, gentle 2–4% push-ins across a frame, power2/power3 eases. Reveal each element on its spoken cue; nothing front-loaded. Holds are still. No bounce, no flashes.
- **Held / breather frames:** Frame 4 (statement, held), Frame 9 (closing line, held), Frame 10 (silent end card, long stillness then fade).
- **Truth rules:** numbers only from the script. Lea's speed trace (Frame 3) is labelled "redrawn from Wilson et al. 2013"; the temperature line (Frame 7) is labelled "schematic". Turning paths (Frame 5) are labelled "illustration".
- **Negative list:** no neon, no dark mode, no glass, no emojis, no Playfair/Barlow/IBM Plex Mono, no stock-photo look, no slideshow front-load, no screensaver floating, nothing important below y=1560 (captions).
