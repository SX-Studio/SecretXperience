## Notes (facts and their sources)

- 242 mph: Frightful, 1999, reportedly (Guinness World Records "fastest bird (diving)"); Guinness's conservative typical stoop ≈200 mph.
- Cheetah top recorded 58 mph (Wilson et al. 2013, from episode 1).
- Faster dives → more aerodynamic force to steer → better catch success vs agile prey (Mills, Hildenbrandt, Taylor & Hemelrijk, PLOS Computational Biology 2018).
- GPS + onboard cameras on peregrines in Wales; attacks follow proportional navigation, the guidance law of most guided missiles (Brighton, Thomas & Taylor, PNAS 2017).
- DDT thinned eggshells; 324 breeding pairs in North America in 1975; listed 1970, delisted 1999 (US Fish & Wildlife Service, Federal Register 1999).
- No gore: the Audubon plate shows prey; crop/mat so blood is not a focal point.
