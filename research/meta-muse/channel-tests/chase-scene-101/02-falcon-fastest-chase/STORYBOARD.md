## Video direction

- **Look:** "Field Journal × Telemetry" (frame.md) + the Episode 2 **Turbo** layer (frame.md addendum). Ivory paper + grid on every frame (own clip layer), public-domain plates, Caveat field notes, compass hairlines, ONE signal red (#C2412D) for data/HUD. Turbo adds speed lines, stat slams, a versus card, a targeting reticle, tiny impact shakes — on action beats only.
- **Two audiences:** every frame has a visible change roughly every 1.5 s (younger retention) but every sentence of on-screen text stays long enough to read (older). Large type (nothing essential under 34px).
- **Motion grammar:** reading beats are calm (power2/3, ink wipes, self-drawing lines); action beats are fast (expo.out slams, streaks). No bounce. Holds are still.
- **Series continuity:** Frame 4 brings back the cheetah ink plate from episode 1 (public/cheetah-ink.png is NOT in this project — draw a simple ink cheetah silhouette or use the word card only).
- **Truth rules:** only the script's numbers. Simulation chart = "schematic". Ship/falcon diagrams = "illustration". Source chips on factual frames.
- **Negative list:** no gore focus (Audubon plate prey cropped/matted out of focus), no neon, no dark mode, no emojis, no Playfair/Barlow/IBM Plex Mono, nothing important below y=1560 (captions).
