## Facts and sources

- Fastest land mammal in North America; 55 mph for about half a mile, and fast for long distances — NPS (Wind Cave, Bryce Canyon) pronghorn pages; Guinness "fastest mammal on land over long distances".
- No living predator matches it; thought to have evolved to outrun the extinct American cheetah (Miracinonyx), extinct ~12,000 years ago — NPS Wind Cave pronghorn page; Byers (1997) "American Pronghorn: Social Adaptations and the Ghosts of Predators Past". The hypothesis is debated, hence "scientists think".
- Wolves ~35–40 mph, coyotes ~40 mph top speed — said as "near forty".
