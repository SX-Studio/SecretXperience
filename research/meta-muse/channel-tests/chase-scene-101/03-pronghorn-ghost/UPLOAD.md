The pronghorn can hit 55 mph and keep running for miles. No predator alive in North America can match it. Scientists think it evolved that speed to escape the American cheetah (Miracinonyx, a cheetah-like cat), which went extinct about 12,000 years ago. The cheetah is gone. The pronghorn never slowed down.
