# Brief: "The Great Molasses Flood" (vertical Short, ~82 s)

Reviewed before generation, against intent, accuracy, style, camera, effects and sound.

## Intent
- **Viewer feeling.** "Wait, what?" in the first 3 seconds. Rising dread through the tank and rivets. A visceral peak at the wave. A quiet, respectful middle for the deaths. A sweet, eerie close.
- **Accuracy rule.** Every number comes from the record:
  - January 15, 1919.
  - 2.3 million US gallons.
  - The tank was 50 ft tall, painted brown to hide leaks.
  - The temperature went from 2°F to over 40°F.
  - The tank burst about 12:30 pm, with sounds like machine-gun fire.
  - The wave was about 25 ft high and moved at about 35 mph.
  - A train car was knocked off the elevated tracks.
  - The molasses thickened as it cooled.
  - 21 dead and about 150 injured.
  - The harbor was brown until summer.
  - Rules on engineers signing off followed.
  - For decades, residents said the area smelled of molasses on hot days.
- **The archival photo.** It is Library of Congress, American National Red Cross collection: "Boston, Mass. Boston molasses explosion", 1919, "No known restrictions on publication." It is credited on screen.
- **No fake masthead.** There is no real newspaper's name or branding. The page says "EXTRA" and "Special Edition" only.

## Style bible
- **Look.** A 1919 front page brought to life, cut with 3D reconstructions lit like old film.
- **Palette:**

  | Role | Color |
  |---|---|
  | Newsprint | #e8dcc4 |
  | Ink | #1b1611 |
  | Molasses | #0e0500, #4a2205, highlight #e0952f |
  | Red ink, used sparingly | #8e1b12 |
  | Steel | #7d8791 |
  | Winter morning | #a9b9c6 |

- **Type:**
  - UnifrakturMaguntia for the masthead "Extra".
  - Playfair Display 900 for headlines.
  - Oswald 700 for numbers.
  - Special Elite for typewriter datelines and credits.
  - Old Standard TT for captions and body text.
- **Texture on every frame:**
  - A paper multiply on the paper scenes.
  - Animated film grain.
  - A 1 to 2 px gate weave.
  - A 2 to 4 percent luminance flicker.
  - Dust specks and the occasional scratch.
  - A warm vignette.
- **Captions.** One line at a time in Old Standard TT bold 56 px, cream on a soft dark band, centered at y≈1530. Words fade in as spoken, typewriter-style, and stay inside the 18 percent bottom safe zone.
- **Motion rules.** Nothing pops. Entrances ease in and out with fade, blur and scale. The camera never stops dead. Hard cuts happen only on the burst and the wave.

## Review fixes applied
- Corrected "train" to "train car" and "elevated tracks", per the record.
- The smell is attributed to "locals swore", because it is a legend.
- The Boston Molasses Co. trademark label was dropped. It is a different company from the tank owner (Purity Distilling) and would imply a false link.
- Scenes 8 and 9 keep the photo after the 3D wave, so the real wreckage lands as proof right after the recreation.
- The ending is held at least 3 s with a long fade and music tail. The first Short's ending felt choppy.
