# Social Ripple: the umbrella, and a season where each episode moves on (2026-11-01)

## The umbrella

The user wants the channel to cover **everything social**, under one giant umbrella. The context should shift and progress with every episode instead of repeating episode 1's "mood between strangers" frame.

The working definition is **how people live together**:
- people and groups, status, money and work;
- cities, institutions, economic and political systems (capitalism, socialism, communism, democracy, the welfare state);
- history's turning points, technology, and the future of society.

Political systems are covered **even-handedly**: how each works, the real-world evidence, and the trade-offs, so viewers think for themselves. That keeps the channel trustworthy and keeps its videos fully advertiser-friendly.

The series device stays the same: **one small act or rule, rippling outward**, now applied to systems as well as people.
