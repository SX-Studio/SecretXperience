## Readings

The samples are tiny, so these are signals, not conclusions.

1. **The channel can't use custom thumbnails yet** (phone verification is pending). Every video is shown with an automatic frame YouTube picked, so our designed thumbnails have never been seen. That alone plausibly explains a CTR of 0.7–2.1%. **Fixing this is priority #1.**
2. **Viewers leave around 24–35 s.** Most traffic is "Suggested", so people arrive from another video and decide within seconds. The first 5 s must show the title's promise. For Ep4, the retention graph (available after about 48 h) will show the exact drop.
3. Episode 2's CTR (2.1%, mostly Browse) is the best so far. Its title had a clear "levels" frame.

**The curve:** flat at about 300 for roughly the first 15 h, then a jump to about 1.6K between hours 15 and 25. YouTube widened its test of the video, mostly through Suggested (80%).

**The math:**

| CTR | Views from 1.8K impressions |
|---|---|
| 0.7% (actual, auto-frame thumbnail) | ~13 |
| 4% (a normal designed-thumbnail CTR) | ~70 |
| 6% | ~110 |

The algorithm is willing to show the video, so the click is the bottleneck. Custom thumbnails (phone verification) are the highest-leverage fix.

**Likely reasons Ep4 got more reach:**
- One high-demand word ("Loneliness") in the "Psychology of [Word]" frame. The topic research found this frame's median is 6× the channel norm.
- A 3–6 minute length.

**Keep for Ep5+:** a "The Psychology of [Word]…" title, with the day-by-day or "what if" story inside.
