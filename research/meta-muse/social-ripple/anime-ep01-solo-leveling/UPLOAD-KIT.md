Research in this video:
• Lally, van Jaarsveld, Potts & Wardle (2010). How are habits formed: Modelling habit formation in the real world. European Journal of Social Psychology, 40, 998–1009. (Median about 66 days to automaticity among well-fitting participants; range 18 to an estimated 254; missing one day did not materially affect habit formation.)
• Maltz, M. (1960). Psycho-Cybernetics. (Source of "a minimum of about 21 days".)
• Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006). Implementation intentions and goal achievement: A meta-analysis. Advances in Experimental Social Psychology, 38, 69–119. (94 tests, 8,000+ participants, d = .65.)
• Amabile & Kramer (2011). The Power of Small Wins (Harvard Business Review) / The Progress Principle. (Nearly 12,000 diary entries from 238 professionals; not peer-reviewed; about work.)
• Wood, Quinn & Kashy (2002). Habits in everyday life. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 83, 1281–1297. (Between a third and a half of behaviours.)
