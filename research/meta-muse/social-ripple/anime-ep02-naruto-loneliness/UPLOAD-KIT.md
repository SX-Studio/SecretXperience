Research in this video:
• Hawkley & Cacioppo (2010). Loneliness matters: A theoretical and empirical review of consequences and mechanisms. Annals of Behavioral Medicine. ("Loneliness is not simply being alone.")
• Baumeister & Leary (1995). The need to belong. Psychological Bulletin. (A review: frequent, non-aversive interactions within an ongoing bond.)
• Eisenberger, Lieberman & Williams (2003). Does rejection hurt? An fMRI study of social exclusion. Science. (Small study, 13 participants; later research debates how similar social and physical pain really are.)
• Holt-Lunstad, Smith & Layton (2010). Social relationships and mortality risk: A meta-analytic review. PLoS Medicine. (148 studies, 308,849 participants; stronger social ties: 50% higher odds of survival; observational.)
• Masi, Chen, Hawkley & Cacioppo (2011). A meta-analysis of interventions to reduce loneliness. Personality and Social Psychology Review. (50 studies; small overall effects; addressing negative social thoughts looked most promising, based on few studies.)
• Epley & Schroeder (2014). Mistakenly seeking solitude. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. (Commuters who talked to a stranger had a more pleasant ride.)
