Someone your age just got the thing you wanted. And you didn't feel happy for them. You felt smaller.

Dragon Ball Z spent 250 episodes on that feeling. Its name is Vegeta. Here's what his rivalry with Goku, and real research, teach us about comparing ourselves to other people, and what to do with it.

If comparing has you feeling worthless, please talk to someone you trust. Free, confidential support:

Research in this video:
• Festinger (1954). A theory of social comparison processes. Human Relations. (A theory paper, not an experiment: we judge our abilities against people near our own level.)
• Kilduff, Elfenbein & Staw (2010). The psychology of rivalry. Academy of Management Journal. (US college basketball: rivalry was strongest between teams that were similar, evenly matched and had met often.)
• Gerber, Wheeler & Suls (2018). A social comparison theory meta-analysis 60+ years on. Psychological Bulletin. (Given the choice, people compare upward, and it usually lowers how they feel about themselves.)
• Kilduff (2014). Driven to win: Rivalry, motivation, and performance. Social Psychological and Personality Science. (Amateur runners ran faster in races featuring their rivals. A correlation, not proof of cause.)
• Kilduff, Galinsky, Gallo & Reade (2016). Whatever it takes to win: Rivalry increases unethical behavior. Academy of Management Journal. (Italian football 2002–2009: same-city matches averaged 6.03 yellow cards against 4.25. In a 70-student experiment, 50% lied to a rival-university partner against 12% to others.)
• van de Ven, Zeelenberg & Pieters (2009, 2011). Leveling up and down; Why envy outperforms admiration. Emotion; Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin. (Two kinds of envy; in small student experiments only the "move up" kind improved effort.)
• Gürel, Brummelman, Sedikides & Overbeek (2020). Better than my past self. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. (583 children and teens: comparing with your past self raised pride without the wish to be superior. Evidence in adults is thin.)

The three steps at the end are our suggestions, not study findings.

Notes for fans: "It's over 9000!" is the English dub line (the Japanese original and the manga say 8,000). "You are number one" is the line in the Japanese original, the manga and the Kai dub; the 2003 English dub says "the best". Scenes discussed: episodes 28–35, 95, 129, 228–229 and 280.

Dragon Ball Z is by Akira Toriyama, anime by Toei Animation. This is an original animated commentary: the story is told in our own words with our own drawings (no footage or official art). Music and sound: original.
