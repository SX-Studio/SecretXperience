# The Social Ripple crew (original characters)

All four are our own designs, rigged in `../shorts/kit/cast.js` (joints, poses, side and front views, walk cycle, push-up IK with planted hands, expressions, blink, breathing, tears that follow the head, lip-sync to narration). Styled with common hairstyles and current streetwear.

| Character | Role | Hair | Outfit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nia** | Host and narrator; warm, curious | Box braids in a high bun | Round glasses, oversized cream half-zip, light-wash wide-leg jeans, chunky white sneakers |
| **Kai** | The grinder: trains, quits, comes back | Low taper fade, curly top | Oversized olive hoodie, black cargo pants, chunky white sneakers |
| **Mo** | The quiet one who feels left out | Middle-part curtain hair | Black hoodie under a grey puffer vest, baggy jeans, headphones round the neck |
| **Rue** | The loyal friend who shows up | Sleek high ponytail, claw clip | Cropped coral puffer over a white tee, light-wash baggy jeans, low-profile sneakers |

Anime characters: when a lesson references an anime moment, that anime's characters appear, drawn by us on the same rig with locked model sheets that match canon (see `naruto-characters-sheet.jpg`): Naruto (Part 1 orange-and-blue jumpsuit; goggles before the headband), Iruka (flak vest, nose scar, ponytail), Gaara (red hair, forehead kanji, dark outfit, white sash, gourd), Haku (hunter-nin mask, green haori with white trim over a brown pinstriped outfit, brown sash, white bun holder, two cuffed front locks). The crew handles hosting, viewer situations, conclusions and summaries.

One Piece set (see `one-piece-characters-sheet.jpg`; canon notes in `../anime-ep03-one-piece-help/RESEARCH.md`, section A3): Luffy (red vest with gold buttons, blue shorts with white fluffy cuffs, sandals, scar under the left eye; the straw hat is an accessory so it can move to Nami, and he is hatless on the walk to Arlong Park), Luffy at Enies Lobby (`luffyEL`: black cargo shorts, hat hanging on his back), Nami at Arlong Park (short orange hair, green-spotted camisole, shoulder tattoo; wears Luffy's hat after "help me"), Zoro at Arlong Park (open navy swirl shirt over chest bandages, green haramaki, one white-hilted sword), Usopp (bandana, goggles, brown overalls, satchel), Sanji (black suit, hair over one eye), Robin at Enies Lobby (black long-sleeved leather dress, thigh-high boots, hands cuffed behind her back), Sogeking (gold sun mask with blue eye band, maroon cape). Continuity rule: these designs are locked; a scene set in a different arc gets its own outfit variant rather than changing these.
