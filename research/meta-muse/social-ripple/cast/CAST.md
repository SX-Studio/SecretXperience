# The Social Ripple crew (original characters)

All four are our own designs, rigged in `../shorts/kit/cast.js` (joints, poses, walk cycle, push-up IK, expressions, blink, breathing, lip-sync to narration).

| Character | Role | Look |
|---|---|---|
| **Nia** | Host and narrator; warm, curious | Curly puff bun, big round glasses, mustard cardigan, red notebook |
| **Kai** | The grinder: trains, quits, comes back | Brown swoop hair, teal hoodie with one torn sleeve, red tally wristband, white sneakers |
| **Mo** | The quiet one who feels left out | Shaggy navy hair over one eye, tired eyes, grey coat, lavender scarf, headphones round the neck |
| **Rue** | The loyal friend who shows up | Red bob with yellow clips, freckles, yellow jacket, backpack straps, bandaged knuckle |

Poses: stand, wave, fist, cheer, point, think, look, slump, pockets, pushUp, pushDown, flat. Expressions: neutral, smile, grin, sad, strain, wow, determined, tired.
