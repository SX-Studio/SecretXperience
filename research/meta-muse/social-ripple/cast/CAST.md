# The Social Ripple crew (original characters)

All four are our own designs, rigged in `../shorts/kit/cast.js` (joints, poses, side and front views, walk cycle, push-up IK with planted hands, expressions, blink, breathing, lip-sync to narration). Styled with common hairstyles and current streetwear.

| Character | Role | Hair | Outfit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nia** | Host and narrator; warm, curious | Box braids in a high bun | Round glasses, oversized cream half-zip, light-wash wide-leg jeans, chunky white sneakers |
| **Kai** | The grinder: trains, quits, comes back | Low taper fade, curly top | Oversized olive hoodie, black cargo pants, chunky white sneakers |
| **Mo** | The quiet one who feels left out | Middle-part curtain hair | Black hoodie under a grey puffer vest, baggy jeans, headphones round the neck |
| **Rue** | The loyal friend who shows up | Sleek high ponytail, claw clip | Cropped coral puffer over a white tee, light-wash baggy jeans, low-profile sneakers |

Anime characters: when a lesson references an anime moment, that anime's characters appear, drawn by us on the same rig (so far: Naruto with goggles/headband, Iruka, Gaara, Haku). The crew handles hosting, viewer situations, conclusions and summaries.
