# Ep7 thumbnail brief: "What If You Could See How Much Everyone Was Secretly Struggling?"
- **Premise in one sentence:** you can suddenly see everyone's real inner weather over their head; they all smile and say "fine", but many have storms.
- **Visual device:** a weather icon over each head (sun / cloud / rain / storm). The contrast between a smiling face and a storm overhead IS the concept.
- **Hero + emotion:** YOU (the viewer stand-in), wide-eyed and surprised: the moment of seeing it.
- **Background crowd (= the topic):** translucent townspeople, all smiling or waving, several saying "I'm fine!" or "Great!" in bubbles, under rain or storm clouds. A few have honest suns.
- **Interface element:** counter "STORMS SEEN".
- **First-person text options:** "THEY SAID FINE." · "I CAN SEE IT." · "NOBODY'S FINE?" · "EVERYONE LIED?"
- **Variant A:** a crowd of smiling people under storms, YOU in the foreground, with "THEY SAID FINE."
- **Variant B:** one face. Ana, the helper, beaming with an "I'm fine!" bubble under a giant storm, with the crowd behind and "SHE'S NOT." (the midpoint reveal).
- **Brand guard:** no shame or fear. The crowd smiles; the storms are gentle cartoon weather, not menace.
