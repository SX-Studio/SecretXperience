# Ep4 — "What 30 Days of Saying Hello Does to a Lonely Person"

Format: MrBeast concept DNA × Social Ripple heart. Host: Nia (on screen + narrator). Crumb Island.

## Promise
One hello a day, for 30 days, to the loneliest person on the island. Proven in 30 s: Day 1 (ignored) vs. a glimpse of Day 30 (he says hello first — to someone else) — not shown in full until the end.

## Devices
- **Counter** (top-left, always): `DAY 07 / 30` with a calendar-page flip, plus `HELLOS 7`.
- **Talk meter** from Day 20: minutes talked, rising.
- **Palette**: Theo's world starts desaturated (cool, grey-blue); colour returns with the days. Rain on Day 16.
- **Lore**: Mo watching from his shop window every day with a sandwich, as a running gag ("Day 3: Mo is invested").

## Retention design (user goal: average view duration ≈ full length)
1. **Tighter runtime: ~7:00, not 8:30.** Every line must earn its place; anything that doesn't move the story, the counter or the payoff is cut.
2. **The payoff is the last thing in the video.** Takeaways move *before* Day 30 (woven into Day 29 night). Day 30 lands at about 6:20. After the reveal there are ≤ 20 s: one closing line and the breathing shot. No "so in conclusion" signposting, no long outro, no "thanks for watching".
3. **Three open loops planted in the first 30 s, closed late:**
   - Will Theo ever say hello back? (closed Day 7, at about 2:30)
   - What's on Theo's wall? (a glimpse of his calendar in the hook, closed at the Day 15 twist)
   - "Something happens on day thirty that neither of them saw coming." (closed at the very end)
4. **A re-hook every 45–60 s:** a new day, a twist, a new question on screen, or a callback ("remember that calendar?").
5. **The midpoint twist sits at about 50%**, where long videos usually sag. A second twist (rain, the empty bench) follows about 50 s later.
6. **The counter is always visible**, so viewers always know how far there is to go and want to see Day 30.
7. **No dead air in the first 60 s.** The host intro is 2 lines max and folded into the stakes.
8. **Humor every ~60 s** (Mo's window gag, the seagull, "the hello lady"), since funny is the biggest driver of likes in our comment research.
9. **End screen:** the final 10–15 s breathing shot doubles as the end-screen space, so viewers aren't cut off mid-scene.
