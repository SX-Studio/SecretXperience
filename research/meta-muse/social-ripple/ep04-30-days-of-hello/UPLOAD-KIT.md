## Description
Theo moved to Crumb Island three months ago. Since then, his longest conversation was with a seagull. So Ana makes a plan: one hello a day, for thirty days. No speeches. No fixing him. Just one hello.

This is an animated story, but every piece of it comes from real research.

If loneliness feels heavy right now, you're not broken. Reaching out to someone you trust, or to a mental-health professional, can help.

Research in this episode:
• US Surgeon General (2023). Our Epidemic of Loneliness and Isolation.
• Cacioppo & Hawkley (2009). Perceived social isolation and cognition. Trends in Cognitive Sciences.
• Epley & Schroeder (2014). Mistakenly seeking solitude. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General.
• Sandstrom & Dunn (2014). Is efficiency overrated? Social Psychological and Personality Science.
• Boothby, Cooney, Sandstrom & Clark (2018). The liking gap in conversations. Psychological Science.
• Liu, Rim, Min & Min (2023). The surprise of reaching out. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.
• Hall (2019). How many hours does it take to make a friend? Journal of Social and Personal Relationships.

Today's tiny experiment: say one hello you'd normally skip.
