## Description
Bea makes the best cinnamon buns on Crumb Island. She also replays things she said four years ago. Most nights.

Then one Monday she wakes up able to hear every honest thought anyone has about her, for seven days. There are three rules. The third one is in very small print.

This is an animated story, but every study in it is real.

If the voice in your head is loud right now, you're not alone. Talking to someone you trust, or to a mental-health professional, can help.

Research in this episode:
• Gilovich, Medvec & Savitsky (2000). The spotlight effect in social judgment. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.
• Gilovich, Savitsky & Medvec (1998). The illusion of transparency. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.
• Savitsky, Epley & Gilovich (2001). Do others judge us as harshly as we think? Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.
• Bruk, Scholl & Bless (2018). Beautiful mess effect. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology.
• Breines & Chen (2012). Self-compassion increases self-improvement motivation. Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin.

Today's tiny experiment: the next time you slip up, answer that voice the way you'd answer a friend who just dropped a tray.
