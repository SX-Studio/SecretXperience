## S01 cold open
n01 (Nia): In two seconds, Bea is going to hear exactly what every one of these people thinks of her.
n02 (Nia): But first, let's go back to Monday.

## S02 setup + rules
n03: This is Bea. She makes the best cinnamon buns on Crumb Island. She also still replays something awkward she said four years ago. Most nights.
n04: And on Monday morning, Bea woke up able to hear every honest thought anyone had about her.
n05: There were three rules. One: if someone thinks about you, you hear it. Two: it lasts seven days. And rule three was written in very, very small print. We'll get to that.

## S03 day 1
n06: Day one. Bea braced herself for a flood.
mo_t1 (Mo, thought): Ooh. Cinnamon.
finn_t1 (Finn, thought): Those buns look incredible.
n07: That was it. All day. Three thoughts. Two of them were about the buns.
n08: Psychologists call this the spotlight effect. We feel like everyone is watching us. In one study, students wore an embarrassing T-shirt into a room [F1: guessed about half would notice; far fewer did].

## S04 day 2
cust1 (customer): Enjoy your day!
bea_01 (Bea): You too! ...Wait.
n09: Day two. Bea replayed that "you too" about forty times.
bea_c1 (Bea's inner voice): He thinks you're so weird.
n10: And what did he actually think?
n11: Nothing. He'd forgotten before he reached the door.
n12: This one's called the illusion of transparency. We assume our nerves and awkward moments are obvious to everyone. Mostly, they can't see them at all.

## S05 day 3 (back to the cold open)
n13: Which brings us back to day three.
kai_t1 (Kai, thought): Oof. That happens to me all the time.
ana_t1 (Ana, thought): Is she okay?
theo_t1 (Theo, thought): I should help.
n14: Researchers have found that we expect people to judge our mistakes much more harshly than they actually do. [F2]
n15: Theo brought a broom. Mo brought a sandwich. Nobody knows why.

## S06 day 4 midpoint
n16: Day four. Bea's mum came to visit.
n17: Bea had always believed her mum was a little disappointed. A bakery. Not the career she'd hoped for.
n18: So Bea listened.
mum_t1 (Mum, thought): I'm so proud of her. I've just never known how to say it.

## S07 day 5-6
n19: Day five. Not every thought was kind.
cust2_t1 (customer, thought): The buns are a bit dry today.
n20: Ouch. And that's real. Some people will think critical things. But notice what it was about. The buns. Not Bea. Small, specific, and honestly, fixable.
n21: Day six. Bea did something brave. She told her mum she missed her.
n22: Showing that kind of vulnerability feels like weakness from the inside. From the outside, people tend to see it as courage. [F4: beautiful mess effect]

## S08 day 7 payoff
n23: Day seven. The quietest day yet. Except for one voice that hadn't stopped all week.
n24: Remember rule three? The very small print?
n25: Every thought about you. Including your own.
bea_c2 (inner voice): They're laughing at you.
bea_c3 (inner voice): You always mess things up.
bea_c4 (inner voice): Why are you like this?
n26: All week, the harshest voice on Crumb Island wasn't a customer. It was Bea.
n27 (Nia on screen): If that voice sounds familiar, you're not alone. Overthinking can feel like a crowd. Try this: talk to yourself the way you'd talk to a friend who just dropped a tray.

## S09 day 8 close
n28: Day eight. The thoughts were gone.
bea_02 (Bea): Oops. ...That's okay.
n29: Most people are thinking about you far less than you think. And the ones who are? They're probably kinder than the voice in your head.
