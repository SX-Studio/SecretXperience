# Ep6: "What If You Could See Every Life You've Ever Touched?"
Inspired by the *premise* of a classic film about seeing your life's impact. Only the premise is borrowed: our own device (golden threads), our cast, our lore. No characters, scenes, names, music or imagery from any film.

Target: 3:30–4:15, about 600 words. Host: Nia (narrator, plus one on-screen takeaway). Lead: **Mo**, who has run Mo's Sandwiches for 22 years. He's the running sandwich gag from Ep5 ("Mo brought a sandwich. Nobody knows why.").

## Lessons applied
| Lesson | Where |
|---|---|
| Emotional image in the first 3 s, premise by 0:10 | Cold open: Mo alone, locking up; golden threads burst out across the whole island |
| 2–3 rules on screen early; a rule that pays off later | Rule 3: "Pull a thread, and that moment never happened" (the mental-subtraction device) |
| Counter | "LIVES TOUCHED" tally climbing as threads are followed |
| Midpoint gut-punch | Mo pulls one thread, and part of the town fades |
| A real study at each step | kindness underestimated · cooperation cascades · mental subtraction · gratitude notes |
| Lore payoff | Theo (Ep4), the broom (Ep5), Bea's buns (Ep5), Kai's Building Co. sign |
| Payoff last, ≤20 s after it | A thread that comes *back* to Mo: Theo's thank-you note |

## Beats
| # | Beat | Device |
|---|---|---|
| S01 | **0:00** Night. Mo locks his shop alone. Nia: "Mo thinks if he disappeared, the island would only miss the tuna melt." Then golden threads burst from his chest, hundreds of them, across the island. "Tonight, he's going to find out exactly how wrong he is." | cold open, open loop |
| S02 | Who Mo is: 22 years, same apron, a life he thinks is small. RULES card: 1. Every thread is a life you've touched. 2. The brighter the thread, the more it mattered to them. 3. Pull a thread, and that moment never happened. ("Rule three comes with a warning.") | rules, loop #2 |
| S03 | The first bright thread leads to Theo. Flashback, 3 months ago: Theo's first week, Mo slides him a free sandwich: "Welcome to the island." Mo forgot it the same day. Theo didn't. **Study: we underestimate how much small kindnesses mean to the person receiving them.** | study 1, lore |
| S04 | The thread doesn't stop at Theo. Theo → the broom for Bea (Ep5) → Bea's extra buns for the lonely neighbour. Counter climbs. **Study: in a lab game, generosity spread to people up to three steps away.** | cascade, study 2 |
| S05 | **Midpoint.** A thick old thread to the harbour. Years ago, on a stormy night, Mo kept the shop open for a soaked stranger with a packed suitcase: Kai. Kai stayed. Kai built half the houses on the island. Mo, curious, pulls the thread (rule 3)… Kai's houses fade, one by one; families, porches and lights wink out. Silence. Mo frantically pushes it back. | gut-punch, rule 3 pays off |
| S06 | **Study: picturing a good thing never happening made people feel happier than picturing it happening, and people predicted the opposite.** Mo looks at the threads differently. | study 3 |
| S07 | Payoff. The threads fade at dawn. Mo opens the shop like any day. The door bell rings: Theo, with a folded note. "I never said thank you." Mo reads it. A thread glows once more, this time pointing *to* Mo. Nia (on screen): you'll never see your threads, but they're there. Try this: tell one person what they did that mattered to you. **Study: people underestimate how good a thank-you note makes the other person feel, and overestimate how awkward it is.** | payoff, takeaway |
| S08 | Close: wide shot of the island at morning; a few faint threads between *everyone*. Final line. ~8 s breathing end for the end screen. | close |
