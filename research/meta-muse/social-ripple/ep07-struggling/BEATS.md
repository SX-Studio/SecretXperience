# Ep7: "What If You Could See How Much Everyone Was Secretly Struggling?"
Format tests (from the Jeremy Bearimy study):
- **Second-person narration**, with a recurring viewer stand-in labelled **YOU** on Crumb Island.
- **Fast cutting:** a new shot every 2–4 s. Hold longer only for the midpoint and the payoff.

Target 3:30–4:00, about 550 words. Narrator: Nia (voice only; one on-screen takeaway). Research claims are locked to RESEARCH.md (verified 2026-11-05). No prevalence statistics on screen; no Huang et al.

## The device (original)
**Inner weather:** a small icon above each head showing how the person *really* feels: sun, cloud, rain, storm. Faces keep smiling; the weather tells the truth.

**Rules card:**
1. You can see everyone's inner weather.
2. They can't see it, and neither can anyone else.
3. Yours is the only one you can't see. (The fine-print payoff: you assume yours is the worst.)

## Thumbnail concept
Busy square, everyone smiling, with labels over their heads: ☀️ "FINE" over a storm, 🌧️ "GREAT!" over rain. Big text: **"THEY'RE NOT OK"**? Too negative? Alternative: **"EVERYONE?"** Pick after the goldfish review; no shame or fear framing.
