## Rules for the whole episode
- **YOU lives on screen left, facing right.** Progress is YOU moving left to right. YOU crosses to screen right once, on camera, at the midpoint, when YOU knocks on Ana's door (SC07). After that, others enter from the left.
- **Every scene turns.** Each card names its value change (from → to). A scene that ends where it started gets cut.
- **One open question always running.** Never more than ~30 s without a new one (loop table at the end).
- **Show it, then name it.** The viewer watches each finding happen before Nia says the verified line. No charts.
- **Weather mix stays honest.** Mostly sun and small clouds, a few rain clouds, at most two storms in any crowd shot. The screen must never imply "most people are struggling".
- **Staging.** Every shot names a preset (P1–P15). Faces stay inside x 0.08–0.92, y 0.06–0.80; captions own the band below y 0.85.
- **Energy.**
  - Hook: ≥1.5 visual events/s.
  - Body: ≥0.8 visual events/s.
  - Before the midpoint, no shot runs over 4 s without a camera move.
  - On-screen characters change expression at least every 4 s.

## Research load
Two main findings and one visual beat; three lines in total plus the authors' framing:
- **J1 (+J3):** we underestimate others' hard times, even close friends do;
- **J4:** we hide bad feelings more than good ones;
- **H1:** more people say yes than we expect when we ask directly;
- **J5:** the authors' framing, at the payoff.

The 40%/13% numbers and the social-media line are dropped. The study numbers are cut back, not the honesty.
