# Ep7 script v2 (after goldfish viewer + claims review): "What If You Could See How Much Everyone Was Secretly Struggling?"
Second person ("you"). The YOU stand-in never speaks; Nia narrates what you do. Claims are locked to RESEARCH.md.

## S01 cold open (busy square; smiles; storm clouds pop over half the heads)
n01 (Nia): Everyone in this square says they're fine.
n02 (Nia): In a second, you'll see the storms they're hiding.

## S02 the morning you start seeing weather + rules
n03: This morning, you woke up and saw weather. Not outside. Over people's heads.
n04: Sun means they're doing fine. Clouds, rain, storms mean they're not. And their faces? Their faces never change.
n05: Three rules. One: you can see everyone's inner weather. Two: they can't. Three: the only weather you can't see is your own.
str_01 (stranger): Morning! Doing great!
n06: He was not doing great.

## S03 montage of hidden weather (lore cameos) + study 1
n07: Once you see it, you see it everywhere.
n08: Bea, the baker, smiling behind her counter. A small cloud. Some days, that inner critic still talks.
n09: Finn, the island's joker, cracking jokes on his boat. Drizzle.
n10: Kai, the builder. Calm, steady, always fine. A storm.
n11: And you'd thought you were the only one having a rough week.
n12: When researchers asked students to guess how many classmates had recently been through something hard, they guessed too low. And the lower the guess, the lonelier they tended to feel.

## S04 why we miss it + study 1A
n13: So why don't we see it?
n14: Because we hide the rain and show the sunshine. In the same research, students kept about forty percent of their bad moments to themselves. Their good moments? Only thirteen percent.
n15: Most feeds are sunny, even when the sky isn't.

## S05 midpoint: Ana
n16: Then you see Ana.
n17: Ana, who said hello to a lonely stranger every day for a month. Ana, who shows up for everyone.
n18: Ana has the biggest storm on the island.

ana_01 (Ana): I'm fine!
n20: And then you ask again. No, really. How are you?

## S07 payoff: rule three
n24: And then Ana does something nobody's done all week.
ana_04 (Ana): What about you? How are you, really?
n25: For the first time, you catch your reflection in the window. Your own weather.
n26: A cloud. Not the biggest. Not the smallest. Just weather, like everyone's.
n27: You were never the only one.

## S08 Nia's takeaway + close
n28 (Nia on screen): You'll never see anyone's weather. But you can ask how someone's doing, and if they say "fine," ask one more time. And if you're the one under the storm, let someone help. They'll probably say yes.
n29: Everyone's carrying some weather. You don't have to carry yours alone.
