## Description
Everyone on Crumb Island says they're fine. Now you can see their weather: sun over the people who are, clouds and storms over the ones who aren't. Their faces never change.

There are three rules. The third one is the one that matters.

This is an animated story, but every study in it is real.

If you're the one under the storm right now, you don't have to carry it alone. Ask someone for help: more people say yes than you'd expect. And if it feels heavy, talking to a mental-health professional can help.

Research in this episode:
• Jordan, Monin, Dweck, Lovett, John & Gross (2011). Misery has more company than people think: Underestimating the prevalence of others' negative emotions. Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin. (People underestimated how often others had hard times, even close friends did; people reported hiding bad moments far more than good ones. Underestimating was linked to feeling lonelier, which is a correlation, not proof of cause.)
• Flynn & Lake (2008). If you need help, just ask: Underestimating compliance with direct requests for help. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. (People guessed they'd need to ask about 20 strangers to get 5 to do a small favour; it took about 10.)

"Ask one more time" is our gentle suggestion, not a study finding.

Today's tiny experiment: ask someone how they're doing. If they say "fine," ask one more time.
