**Not claimed:** that lies can be spotted from body language; that liars are bad people; any number for "lies per day" beyond the papers. Our takeaway, "notice your own little ones", is labelled as our suggestion.

**Tone guardrails:** no shaming. Everyone tells little lies, and the hero has a number too. The prolific liar's lies turn out to be told for someone else (a surprise party). That's fiction illustrating D1's "for someone else's sake" finding and S3's "bad lie days", without claiming every prolific liar is kind.
