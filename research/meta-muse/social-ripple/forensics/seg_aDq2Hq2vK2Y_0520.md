### 1. Monetization Eligibility Rules (YouTube & TikTok)

*   **YouTube Partner Program (Current Thresholds):**
    *   1,000 subscribers + 4,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months.
    *   *OR* 1,000 subscribers + 10 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days.
*   **YouTube Partner Program (Upcoming Changes - Starting Feb. 1, 2027):**
    *   Long-form watch hours threshold increases to **8,000 qualified watch hours** in the last 365 days.
    *   Shorts threshold increases to **20 million qualified Shorts views** in the last 90 days (along with still needing 1k subscribers).
*   **TikTok Creator Rewards Program:**
    *   Videos must be at least **1 minute long**.
    *   Requires **at least 10,000 authentic followers**.
    *   Requires **at least 100,000 authentic total video views** in the last 30 days.
