### 6. 12 Actionable Recommendations
1.  **Adopt a "Seasonal" Narrative:** Don't just make one-off explainers. Create 3-part stories where a "What If" scenario explores a character's life (e.g., from Kindergarten to the World Cup).
2.  **Lean into "Scientific Reframing":** Instead of saying "be disciplined," say "condition your cost-benefit center (dACC)." Audiences love feeling like they are "upgrading a system" rather than fixing a flaw.
3.  **Acknowledge AI (Or Hide the "Scent"):** If using AI, fix the "emdashes," remove "AI-coded" pauses in narration, and manually replace generic synthesized sound effects (like applause) with real ones.
4.  **Use Visual "Subtitle Tricks":** When a character has a negative thought, use the captions to show a positive reframe. Viewers called this "elevated."
5.  **Create "Intersection" Characters:** Give your stick figures distinct accessories (a specific jacket, a hat, a beard). Re-use them across different topics to build a "Social Ripple Universe."
6.  **"Shrink the Entry" for the Viewer:** Start videos with an "uncomfortably accurate" 40-second scenario (e.g., hitting the snooze button) to make them feel "seen" immediately.
7.  **Summarize Your Value:** Always include a 5-point list at the end of psychological/social videos. Viewers copy-paste these into comments, which boosts the algorithm.
8.  **Avoid "The Vague-Post Trap":** Ensure your characters communicate. If an inventor doesn't explain his invention, the audience will turn against the character, not the villain.
9.  **End on Heart, Not Just Success:** Don't end a video on the character being "rich." End on a "real-world connection" (e.g., helping their mother or a friend).
10. **Incentivize "Check-ins":** In "therapeutic" videos, invite viewers to comment their "Day 1" and come back. This builds a recurring community.
11. **Check Your Pronunciation:** If using AI voice, manually check "learn-ed" vs "learned" and other phonetic traps that trigger "AI slop" accusations.
12. **Leverage "Dopamine Downregulation":** Explain social problems (like phone addiction) as biological "overloading" rather than moral weakness. This creates the "healing" vibe your channel seeks.
