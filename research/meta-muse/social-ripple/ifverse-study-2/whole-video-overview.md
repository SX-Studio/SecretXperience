### 2. VFX INVENTORY
*   **Portals / Rifts (The Door):** Yellow-bordered, jagged lightning-tear vortexes that appear in space; shrink over time when objects pass through.
*   **Glows & Golden Lights:** Golden coins, lantern flames flickering (constant, non-flickering in the void vs normal flames), and glowing humanoid static entities made of floating geometric paper shards.
*   **Explosions & Impact Effects:** Cartoon smoke and spark bursts when planes/boats pass through portals or crash into the barrier.
*   **Freezing / Stasis Shards:** Shattered glass/paper textures representing frozen entities or the protagonist breaking through barriers.
*   Frequency: VFX appear dynamically based on narrative beats (every few seconds during action/rules explanations, and continuously as ambient floating debris).
