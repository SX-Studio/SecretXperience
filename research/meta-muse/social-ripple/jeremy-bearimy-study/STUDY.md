## How the 1.3M video works (3:38)
- **Format:** 3D stick figures, glowing threads between people, a floating counter as a HUD.
- **Narration:** second person ("you").
- **Opening:** starts mid-action, with the power visible in the first 5 seconds; rules by 0:40.
- **Cutting:** a new shot every 1.5–3 seconds.
- **Science:** 10–15 second beats (liking gap, halo effect) woven into the story.
- **Structure:**
  - Escalation: wins, then fame.
  - Cost: the counter becomes an obsession and friendships strain.
  - Climax: a choice, where a faint thread that seemed unimportant pays off.
- **Thumbnails:** bright illustrated art, a recurring protagonist, 1–2 huge words, labels over heads ("friend / CRUSH / friend").

## What to adopt
1. Use second-person "What If You…?" titles. Ep6 already does.
2. Cut faster: a new shot every 2–4 seconds (ours were 5–10).
3. Test second-person narration in one episode, with a recurring viewer stand-in on Crumb Island.
4. Use labels over heads in thumbnails.
5. Start mid-action, with the rules by 0:30.
6. Choose premises from the "see something hidden" and social-wish families, with our calm, research-backed payoff.

## What not to adopt
- Their premises, their protagonist design, their art style.
- Panic and scream sequences, and constantly ticking numbers (they work against the calm, therapeutic voice).
- Shock topics.

## Episode ideas that fit both
- **What If Every Nice Thing You Thought About Someone Was Said Out Loud?** Compliments study (Zhao & Epley 2021, already verified for Ep6).
- **What If You Could See How Much Everyone Was Secretly Struggling?** Jordan et al. (2011), "misery has more company than people think". To verify.
- **What If You Could Replay Any Conversation?** Rumination and regret research. To verify.
