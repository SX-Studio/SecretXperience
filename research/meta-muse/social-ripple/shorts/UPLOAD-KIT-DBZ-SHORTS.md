## Descriptions

**sh11: scouter**
```
You can't stop comparing yourself. Vegeta couldn't either, and the reason isn't weakness: it's how minds measure.

Research: Festinger (1954), A theory of social comparison processes (a theory, not an experiment). Gerber, Wheeler & Suls (2018), Psychological Bulletin: given the choice, people compare upward, and it usually lowers how they feel.

"It's over 9000!" is the English dub line; the Japanese original says 8,000 (episode 28). Dragon Ball Z is by Akira Toriyama, anime by Toei Animation. Original animated commentary with our own drawings.

If comparing has you feeling worthless, please talk to someone you trust. Find a free helpline: https://findahelpline.com (US: call or text 988)

#DragonBallZ #Vegeta #Over9000 #Psychology #AnimeLifeLessons
```

**sh12: rival**
```
A rival can push you or poison you. Vegeta lived both: Super Saiyan because of Goku, then Majin Vegeta. The difference is what you want.

Research: Kilduff (2014), Social Psychological and Personality Science: amateur runners ran faster in races featuring their rivals (a correlation). Kilduff, Galinsky, Gallo & Reade (2016), Academy of Management Journal: in a 70-student experiment, 50% lied to a rival-university partner against 12% to others.

Scenes: episodes 129 and 228–229. Dragon Ball Z is by Akira Toriyama, anime by Toei Animation. Original animated commentary with our own drawings.

#DragonBallZ #Vegeta #MajinVegeta #Psychology #AnimeLifeLessons
```

**sh13: number one**
```
Someone is better than you. It took Vegeta 250 episodes to admit that without breaking. Better than you doesn't mean you're worth less.

Research: Gürel, Brummelman, Sedikides & Overbeek (2020), Journal of Experimental Psychology: General: in 583 children and teens, comparing with your past self raised pride without the wish to be superior. Evidence in adults is thin.

"You are number one" is the line in the Japanese original, the manga and the Kai dub; the 2003 English dub says "the best" (episode 280). Dragon Ball Z is by Akira Toriyama, anime by Toei Animation. Original animated commentary with our own drawings.

If comparing has you feeling worthless, please talk to someone you trust. Find a free helpline: https://findahelpline.com (US: call or text 988)

#DragonBallZ #Vegeta #Goku #Psychology #AnimeLifeLessons
```
