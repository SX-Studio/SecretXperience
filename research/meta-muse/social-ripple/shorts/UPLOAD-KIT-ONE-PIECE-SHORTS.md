## Descriptions

**sh08: two words**
```
Nami carried her village alone for eight years. Two words changed it: "help me." And real people say yes more often than we expect.

Study: Flynn & Lake (2008), Journal of Personality and Social Psychology. Students guessed they'd need to ask about 20 strangers to get 5 surveys filled in; it took about 10. Small favors, strangers, small samples.

One Piece is by Eiichiro Oda, anime by Toei Animation (scene: episode 37). Original animated commentary with our own drawings.

If what you're carrying is heavy, please talk to someone you trust. Find a free helpline: https://findahelpline.com (US: call or text 988)

#OnePiece #Nami #AskingForHelp #Psychology #AnimeLifeLessons
```

**sh09: burden**
```
Robin was so afraid of being a burden that she tried to give herself up. Her crew came anyway. Research says the people you ask are more willing, and far less bothered, than you expect.

Study: Zhao & Epley (2022), Psychological Science. People asked a stranger to take their photo; strangers were more willing and less inconvenienced than the askers predicted. Small, quick favors.

One Piece is by Eiichiro Oda, anime by Toei Animation (scene: episode 278). Original animated commentary with our own drawings.

If what you're carrying is heavy, please talk to someone you trust. Find a free helpline: https://findahelpline.com (US: call or text 988)

#OnePiece #NicoRobin #AskingForHelp #Psychology #AnimeLifeLessons
```

**sh10: knock**
```
The people who would help you can't see how hard it is to ask. So they wait, and you wait. The door is usually open, but someone has to knock.

Study: Bohns & Flynn (2010), Journal of Experimental Social Psychology. Peer advisors expected about 13 students to come to them for help; fewer than 8 did.

One Piece is by Eiichiro Oda, anime by Toei Animation (scenes: episodes 37 and 278). Original animated commentary with our own drawings.

If what you're carrying is heavy, please talk to someone you trust. Find a free helpline: https://findahelpline.com (US: call or text 988)

#OnePiece #AskingForHelp #Psychology #AnimeLifeLessons #SocialRipple
```
