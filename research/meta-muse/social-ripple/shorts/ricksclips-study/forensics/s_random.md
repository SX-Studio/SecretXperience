### 1. Script & Narration
**Full Transcript:**
"If you ever fake a number, there's a way to tell a human made it up. A man stole two million dollars with fake checks, made every amount look totally random, and still got caught. But how? Well, picture a building with 19 floors. Step into the elevator and look at the buttons. Floors two through nine each start with a different number. But floor one and ten all the way to 19 all start with a one. That’s more than half the buttons. Real numbers work like that elevator, so ones show up way more than anything else. On top of that, real randomness is streaky. Flip a coin a hundred times and you'll usually get six heads in a row. His checks mostly began with a seven, eight, or nine, and he avoided doubles to make them look random. But truly random numbers are the opposite. So, no matter how random you try to make yours, there’s almost always a way to tell."

**Formula:**
*   **Hook:** Counter-intuitive claim about faking numbers.
*   **Setup:** The $2M fraud case.
*   **Twist/Explanation:** The Elevator Analogy (Benford’s Law).
*   **Payoff:** Why the fraudster’s "random" numbers looked fake.
*   **Loop:** Closes with "a way to tell," leading back to the start.
