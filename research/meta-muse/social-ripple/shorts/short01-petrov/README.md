# Short 01: Stanislav Petrov (46.5 s, 1080×1920)

**Facts (verified on Wikipedia and Britannica, 2026-11-01):**
- 26 Sep 1983, just after midnight, at Serpukhov-15 near Moscow.
- The Oko satellite system reported one US missile launch, then five in all.
- Petrov judged it a false alarm because a real first strike would involve hundreds of missiles and ground radar showed nothing.
- The cause was sunlight on high-altitude clouds.
- He was first praised, then reprimanded for improper paperwork (no diary entry). The story became public in 1998.

The popular "15 minutes" claim is left out because it isn't well sourced.
