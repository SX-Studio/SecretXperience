# ConChaser study (2026-11-07): the same "What If You Could See…?" format as ours, 18K subscribers

Thumbnails and channel data were read from their public channel page. One video was studied by Gemini from its YouTube link (directional; YouTube's bot check blocks downloads from this machine). Their thumbnails are kept here for study only. We never copy their art, character or wording.

## Channel facts
- **Uploads:** 15 videos in the last 15 days (about one long video a day on the Videos tab), each 10–18 minutes. No Shorts were seen.
- **Overlap:** they have already made "What If You Could See How Many Lives Everyone Has Saved?", which is close to our Ep6.

## Thumbnail formula (from 15 thumbnails)
1. **One recurring mascot** in every thumbnail (theirs: a dark-faced figure in a yellow hoodie), recognisable from the feed. *We need our own signature, never theirs.*
2. **Two characters, big**: faces take about 30–50% of the height. There's an exaggerated feeling (side-eye, smug, sweat drop, shock) and nothing else in focus.
3. **The superpower made visible:** glowing neon numbers or labels above heads with small arrows (99 IQ vs 148 IQ, 0 vs 999+ saved, $150 vs $8.7B).
4. **Irony / comparison:** the label contradicts expectations (the janitor saved 999+, the homeless man will be worth billions, "1 DAY LEFT" over a smiling grandma).
5. **Minimal text:** 1–3 tokens, usually numbers. They never repeat the title.
6. **Cinematic light:** golden-hour or window light, rim light, teal/orange contrast, shallow depth of field (blurred background crowd), rich saturation. These are clearly rendered or AI-generated images.

## Video craft (dlJ_roZ_fAM, Gemini, directional)
- **Hook:** the rule in the first 5 s ("a number floating above everyone's head"), then they say what it isn't, then the protagonist's own number (a red 0).
- **Structure:** one mystery carries the whole video. Why does the janitor have 999+ when the famous surgeon has 312? Investigation → backstory twist → public payoff → emotional climax → a quiet moral.
- **Timestamp headers** ("7:30 AM, Monday"), second-person narration, a warm male voice at roughly 140 words per minute, small humour lines.
- **Pacing:** a new shot every 2–4 s, adaptive music (curious → tense → sad strings → triumphant), and UI sounds when numbers appear.
- **Ending:** the protagonist accepts his own 0 and does a small kindness.

## What this means for us
- **Thumbnails:** adopt rules 2–6 now (see `ep07-struggling/remake/thumbnail-C-v2-cinematic-rules-1280x720.jpg`, built in our paper style). Getting the fully rendered look needs an image model. Our Gemini key has no image quota on the free tier.
- **Episodes:** one central mystery per episode, a protagonist we follow, and numbers or labels that appear on screen.
- **Length:** consider 8–12 min once retention holds. They run 10–18 min.
