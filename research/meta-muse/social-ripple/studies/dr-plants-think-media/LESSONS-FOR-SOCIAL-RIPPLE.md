# Dr. Plants (about 6M subs from 12 videos): what Social Ripple adopts

## What he does (from the podcast)
- **Story beats spectacle.** People fall in love with characters, not subjects. 84M people watched an ant video without ever searching for ants.
- **Map the viewer's emotions, not just the plot.** His ant video: sympathy (one lonely ant) → awe (it builds a colony) → tension (war) → excitement (victory). The video should also shift how the viewer sees the world.
- **Hide the lesson inside the plot.** "After the war, they built a graveyard" places a fact at an emotional moment instead of reading it out as a list.
- **The idea is the biggest lever.** He brainstorms 500–1,000 ideas in a week of lock-in, then scores them:
  - Is it original?
  - Is there a story arc?
  - Can you explain it in one sentence?
  - Will it still matter in 100 years?
  - Does it appeal to everyone?
  - Does it shift how people see the world?
  - Is it fun to make?
- **Quality over consistency** if the goal is views (about 3 videos a year). For a business: weekly "maintenance" videos plus one monthly "reach" video.
- **Sound matters more than picture.** He makes his own foley (ripping cabbage for growing plants) and licenses real songs through Lickd. Final check: mute the voiceover; does the edit still hold with music and sound effects alone?
- **Test on people who DON'T care about the topic.** Hand strangers the phone: if it comes back after 10 seconds, the opening fails. Screen-share the cut on FaceTime; where several people drop at the same second, fix it (one fix was colour: blue → yellow). Watch their faces: looking away means tighten; looking confused means clarify.
- **Packaging:** titles under 52 characters; story titles beat search-style titles; brighter intros hold viewers better.
- **The 10% rule:** 10% better than average gives about 10× the views, because AI made most content average.
- **Study films (e.g., Nolan), not just YouTubers.** He writes out the scripts of gripping videos by hand.
- **Be willing to scrap a video** that doesn't hit its emotional beats. Spend "an egregious amount of time" on pre-production.
