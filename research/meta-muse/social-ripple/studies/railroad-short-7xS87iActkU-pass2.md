### G. Script Writing Techniques

1. **Hook**: Begins immediately in *medias res* with a bizarre, counter-intuitive action ("sawing gaps in a rail").
2. **Concrete Specs**: Uses exact units ("every 39 feet", "finger-wide gap") to establish believable engineering detail.
3. **Pacing Conflict**: Introduces a clear high-stakes race ("two companies", "winner gets all").
4. **Misdirection**: Makes the protagonist appear to be making a mistake when his train is mocked as "Clickety-Clack".
5. **Payoff/Twist**: Educates the audience on thermal expansion through dramatic narrative resolution without explicitly sounding like a textbook.
