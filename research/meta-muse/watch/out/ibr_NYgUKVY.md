### 3. Step-by-Step UI Actions

* **[01:44] Artlist.io Browsing**: Selected category filters (*Instrument* $\rightarrow$ *Acoustic Guitar*; *Mood* $\rightarrow$ *Hopeful*), clicked play preview on waveform tracks.
* **[04:47] Premiere Pro (Syncing Hooks)**: Located beat drop waveform spike on track A2, sliced audio at the beat marker, snapped beat drop to video hold point, and extended pre-beat music backward to fill the hook section.
* **[07:15] Premiere Pro (Crossfading)**: Placed second music track on track A2 directly below track A1, aligned beat 1 markers, dragged volume keyframe envelopes to create a diagonal crossfade (fade out A1, fade in A2).
* **[11:18] Premiere Pro (Sub-frame Precision)**: Right-clicked timeline timecode ruler, selected `Show Audio Time Units` to zoom past standard frame boundaries and trim audio exact to waveform transient peaks.
